---
name: check-soundness
description: Use after merging several inputs, views, or findings into one conclusion, before relying on it. Tests whether that synthesis holds together, with every input honored and its parts consistent with one another, and gives a clear verdict.
---

## Steps

### 1. Identify the synthesis

Target: `$ARGUMENTS`, else the synthesis or conclusion in the current context.

When no synthesis was passed, read "the current context" as the page: the last reply, or the document in hand. Note which inputs the synthesis claims to combine and whether they are on the page too. Where the page holds no synthesis, ask for one and stop.

### 2. Map the load-bearing claims

Which assertions does the synthesis depend on? What would break if any proved false? Which claims support multiple parts of the conclusion?

### 3. Check for dropped inputs

Does each original perspective or piece of evidence appear? Was anything dismissed where it should have been integrated, or simplified to the point of distortion? Where the inputs are absent from the page, say so, and check only the inputs the synthesis itself cites.

### 4. Test for internal contradictions

Does any part contradict another? Does the unified view require believing incompatible things? Does resolving one tension create new ones?

### 5. Challenge from multiple angles

What would a skeptic question? What would someone in each original position object to? Where would a domain expert find gaps?

### 6. Verify structural integrity

Does removing one piece cause collapse? Does it depend on unvalidated assumptions? Does the organizing principle actually unify what it claims to?

### 7. Identify brittleness

What new evidence would invalidate it? Which assumptions are least tested? Where is it strongest and where most vulnerable?

### 8. Render verdict

State whether the synthesis passes or fails the coherence test, with specific reasons and any caveats.

## Return

Return the load-bearing claims, each finding from steps 3 to 7 beside the passage it concerns, and the verdict with its reasons and caveats. A step that finds nothing says so in one line. The run is done when the verdict is stated.
