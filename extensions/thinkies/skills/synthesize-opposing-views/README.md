# synthesize-opposing-views

Find higher understanding through dialectic. States thesis and antithesis, examines the conflict, identifies deeper truth, and constructs a synthesis that transcends the either/or framing.

## When it helps

- Two positions each sound right and the argument between them keeps going in circles.
- You suspect the two sides are answering different questions.
- You want a position of your own that keeps what each side got right, not a split down the middle.

## What it hands back

Each side at its strongest, with what it explains well; where exactly they contradict and what each one misses; the deeper point the conflict reveals; a synthesis stated as a position; and a test of that synthesis, including the new questions it raises.

Here it is on "Board games should be played by the rules in the box" versus "house rules are what make a game ours.", cut down ([full run](https://github.com/synapseradio/ai-skills/blob/main/examples/thinkies/synthesize-opposing-views/house-rules.md)):

> - The thesis is answering: what makes a game comparable, teachable, and fair between people who do not know each other?
> - The antithesis is answering: what makes a game valuable to the people who play it repeatedly?
>
> […]
>
> - "Ours" needs a "before". A group cannot make something its own without a thing to make its own.
>
> […]
>
> **Position: the box rules are the baseline that a table earns the right to depart from, by knowing what it is departing from, agreeing on the departure, and being open about it.**

