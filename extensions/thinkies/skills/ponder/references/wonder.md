### 1. Notice the surprising

Notice what seems surprising or counterintuitive about the situation: what would be expected, and what happens instead.

### 2. Ask generative questions

Ask "what if," "what might happen," "how might." Favor a question whose answer you cannot yet guess.

### 3. Follow the threads

Follow interesting threads, letting one question lead to another: answer each provisionally, then ask what that answer raises.

### 4. Treat confusion as signal

Treat confusion as information about what deserves attention. Name each thing that refuses to make sense, and what it points at.

### 5. Start from uncertainty

Use uncertainty as a starting point, not an obstacle. Close on the questions worth carrying forward.
