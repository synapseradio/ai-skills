# Fit index

Read this whole after Diagnose, and read every reference whose condition holds for this piece. Each reference opens with the same `Fits when` line as its entry here. On every revision request, reread this index against the updated alignment state.

## Every run

- [align.md](./align.md) — the wheel of eight rings, the alignment conversation, the two-sketch test, the direction rule, and where state is kept
- [baseline.md](./baseline.md) — ten ring questions and four authorship questions every draft answers, and the authorship line
- [rubric-questions.md](./rubric-questions.md) — questions beyond the baseline, one section per ring

## By fit

- [across-languages.md](./across-languages.md) — Fits when: the piece crosses rhetorical traditions, or a first language other than the piece's own shapes its prose
- [activate.md](./activate.md) — Fits when: the draft names actions whose actor the audience needs to know
- [analogize.md](./analogize.md) — Fits when: the audience meets an idea that nothing in its own experience yet holds
- [arc.md](./arc.md) — Fits when: the piece has to hold its audience from start to end, as a story, a speech, an essay, or a pitch does
- [arrange.md](./arrange.md) — Fits when: the piece has sections or parts whose order is still open to choice
- [bound-scope.md](./bound-scope.md) — Fits when: the piece makes general claims its audience will weigh or act on
- [calibrate.md](./calibrate.md) — Fits when: what the audience already knows is uncertain, or the audience is mixed
- [clarify.md](./clarify.md) — Fits when: the audience meets terms, concepts, or steps it has not met before
- [clarify-patterns.md](./clarify-patterns.md) — Fits when: clarify fits and its pass needs detection heuristics or repair tables
- [dimensionalize.md](./dimensionalize.md) — Fits when: feedback or a judgment word, such as clear or polished, bundles several distinct concerns
- [extract-implications.md](./extract-implications.md) — Fits when: the piece makes claims, rules, or promises that commit its audience or its writer to more than they state
- [fit.md](./fit.md) — Fits when: the form of the piece is still open, or its shape and its audience's purpose may pull apart
- [frame-guide.md](./frame-guide.md) — Fits when: the work starts from a blank page, with no draft and no outline
- [illustrate.md](./illustrate.md) — Fits when: the piece rests on abstract ideas or category words its audience must recognize in practice
- [pose-questions.md](./pose-questions.md) — Fits when: the piece means to open an inquiry, or closes a question it could leave to its audience
- [register.md](./register.md) — Fits when: word choice or tone is at stake, or the user points out prose that reads as machine-made
- [rhythm.md](./rhythm.md) — Fits when: the piece will be heard or read aloud, its sound matters, or the user points out prose that reads as machine-made
- [signal-confidence.md](./signal-confidence.md) — Fits when: the piece makes claims its audience will weigh or act on
- [strengthen.md](./strengthen.md) — Fits when: the draft carries hedges, distancing verbs, abstract nouns, set phrases, or certainty words; its hedge and certainty sections apply where the piece makes claims its audience will weigh or act on
- [surface-assumptions.md](./surface-assumptions.md) — Fits when: the piece argues toward a conclusion its audience will weigh or act on
- [units-and-ledger.md](./units-and-ledger.md) — Fits when: more than two levels of unit are live in the request, or the work runs past one sitting
- [voice.md](./voice.md) — Fits when: who speaks, and from where, is open or shifts across the piece
