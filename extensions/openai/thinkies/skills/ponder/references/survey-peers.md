### 1. What role does the subject fill, and at what grain?

Name the question it answers or the job it does, and the level of detail it sits at.

### 2. What else fills that role at that grain?

List the peers, including ones from outside the current domain. Keep every peer at the subject's grain: no parts of it, no systems containing it, no classes above it.

### 3. What most separates each peer from the subject?

Name one property per peer.

### 4. How do the peers arrange?

Place them along the one or two differences that separate them most.

### 5. Where are the empty positions?

Name each position in the arrangement that nothing occupies, and say whether it is empty by accident or for a reason.

### 6. Where does the subject sit?

Report its position among the peers and its nearest neighbours.
