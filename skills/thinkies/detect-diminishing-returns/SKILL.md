---
name: detect-diminishing-returns
description: Use during research, review, or exploration to decide whether to keep going. Judges whether further effort still changes the picture, and states what has settled and what more work would buy.
---

The work under test is a series of rounds of one kind: searches, drafts, interviews, passes of analysis. Take the series the request names, or, when it names none, the rounds visible in the conversation. With one round or none, say there is no series to judge yet and stop.

## Steps

### 1. Track pattern emergence

For each round, note which themes or changes first appeared in it. Say whether later rounds brought new dimensions or reinforced what was already there.

### 2. Test predictive power

Predict what the next round will show, then check the prediction against it. For rounds already done, predict each from the ones before it. A model of the work that keeps predicting correctly has little left to learn from more of the same.

### 3. Measure theme stability

Compare the early statement of each theme with its current one. Say whether the core holds or new rounds keep reshaping it.

### 4. Count novel information

For each round, count what was new against every earlier round and what only confirmed. Novelty near zero across the recent rounds is the signal.

### 5. Check boundary conditions

Try the edge cases on purpose. Where the existing patterns explain them, the picture holds. Where they expose a gap, name the gap and say whether another round of the same kind could fill it.

### 6. Declare convergence

State what has stabilized, how much confidence the settled part supports, and what one more round of the same kind would add. Convergence shows the work has stopped changing. Whether the work is right is a separate question, so name the questions that only a different kind of step could answer.

## Return

Return a verdict: continue, stop, or switch to a different kind of step. Give with it the per-round evidence from steps 1 to 5 and the open questions from step 6. The run is done when the verdict is stated.
