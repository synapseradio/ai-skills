Winter kitchen

Frost on the pane. Frost on the pane.
My mother's hands, flour to the wrist,
and the radio low, a voice from somewhere warm.

Salt. Yeast. The small bell of the timer.
She never said she was tired.
She said: the dough knows when.

Frost on the pane. The dough knows when.
I set the bowl where she set it.
The kitchen keeps its weather.
