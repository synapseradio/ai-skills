### 1. Identify load-bearing variables

Name the load-bearing variables in the claim or framing: the terms and quantities whose change would change whether it holds.

### 2. Push to extremes

Maximize and minimize each variable to its extreme, and say whether the claim still holds there.

### 3. Test absence

Remove each variable entirely, and say what is left of the claim.

### 4. Change kind, not just degree

Transform the scenario categorically: swap each variable for something of another kind, and say whether the claim still applies.

### 5. Stress the framing itself

Take every key word in the problem statement and substitute alternatives, to check whether the framing constrains thinking unnecessarily.

### 6. Examine the failures

For each boundary where the conclusion failed, say why. Look for one cause behind several failures.

### 7. Revise the scope

Restate the claim with the limits within which it holds.
